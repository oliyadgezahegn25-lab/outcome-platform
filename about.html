01 · OUR JOURNEY

From a focused beginning to a wider supply network.

Established in January 2017 with modest resources and a committed team, Labaid Trading PLC has expanded the scope of its business over the years. We have developed connections with overseas manufacturers, helping bring their products to the Ethiopian market.

Today, Labaid Trading PLC is an importer and distributor of laboratory equipment, chemicals and reagents for universities, educational institutions and research centres throughout Ethiopia. Our wider product interests also include agricultural products and teaching-aid materials.

JANUARY 2017 · THE BEGINNING

The company was established in Addis Ababa with a focus on building a dependable supply business.

GROWTH · EXPANDING OUR REACH

Our business activities broadened, and we opened market access for products from overseas manufacturers.

TODAY · SUPPORTING INSTITUTIONS
