Quick answers to the things organizations often want to know before getting started.

What types of equipment does Labaid supply?+

We supply laboratory, scientific, medical, IT, electrical, power and technical equipment across our listed product areas.

Can Labaid help if I only have a specification?+

Yes. You can send an item name, technical specification, model, catalogue reference or other requirement for review.

Yes. We support requirements from universities, hospitals, research institutions, government organizations, NGOs and other organizations.

Can you help with installation and technical support?+

Where applicable to the supplied equipment, support can include installation, commissioning, training and after-sales coordination.

How do I request a quotation?+

Use the contact page and send the product or requirement details you have, including quantity and specification when available.
